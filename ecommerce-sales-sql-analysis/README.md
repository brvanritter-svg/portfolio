# Ecommerce Sales Analysis

## Project Objective
Analyze e-commerce sales data to provide insights on revenue trends, product performance, and regional profitability.

## Dataset
- Columns: order_date, product_name, category, region, quantity, sales, profit
- Source: Kaggle - [Ecommerce Sales Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/uzmaakhtar/ecommerce-sales-data)

## Questions

### Revenue & Trends
1. What is total sales by month?
2. What is total profit by month?
3. What is month-over-month sales growth?
4. What is the average order sales value?
5. Which month had the highest profit?

### Product Performance
6. Top 10 products by total sales?
7. Top 10 products by total profit?
8. Which categories generate the most revenue?
9. Which categories are most profitable?
10. Which products have high sales but low profit?

### Regional Performance
11. Which region generates the most sales?
12. Which region has the highest profit margin?
